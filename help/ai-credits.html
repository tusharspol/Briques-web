How AI credits work

← All help articles

Anything that uses AI in Briques — generating a tracker, editing a screen, a chat tweak — spends credits. Each plan includes a monthly allowance, and you can top up any time.

Roughly what things cost

  • Generating a new tracker: ~40 credits on the Fast model.
  • Editing one screen: ~10 credits.
  • A small chat tweak: ~3 credits.

Every AI action shows an estimate before it runs and the actual cost after, so there are no surprises.

Monthly allowance

  • Free: 80 credits per month.
  • Pro: 200 credits per month, rolling over up to two months.

Running out

If you run low, buy a one-time credit pack from inside the app: +200 ($2.99), +800 ($9.99), or +2,500 ($24.99). Pack credits never expire and stay yours through cancellation or downgrade.

No surprise bills: Credits cap your AI spend at your plan's allowance. Storage and bandwidth aren't billed separately.
