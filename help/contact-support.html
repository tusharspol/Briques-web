Contact support

← All help articles

A real person reads every message and replies within about two days. Pick the address that fits:

Who to email

  • Something's broken or not working — support@briques.app
  • Privacy, data export, or account deletion — privacy@briques.app
  • Anything else — hello@briques.app

Help us help you faster

When you write in about a problem, it helps to include:

  • What you were doing when it happened.
  • The tracker or screen involved.
  • Your device and OS version (for example, iPhone 14, iOS 18).
  • A screenshot, if you have one.

You can also reach us from the contact page.

Still need help?

Can't find what you need? Email us and a human replies within about two days.
