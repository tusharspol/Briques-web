Export your data

← All help articles

Your data is yours, and you can take it with you. Briques exports your account data in a portable, machine-readable format (JSON).

Request an export

Email privacy@briques.app from your account's email address and ask for a data export. We respond within 30 days — usually much sooner — with a file you can download.

What's included

  • Your account details.
  • Your tracker definitions (their structure and logic).
  • The records stored inside your trackers.
Before you delete: If you're planning to delete your account, request your export first — deletion is permanent.
