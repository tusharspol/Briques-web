Plans & billing

Manage or cancel your subscription

← All help articles

Your Briques Pro subscription is billed by the app store you bought it from — Apple on iOS, Google on Android. That's also where you change or cancel it.

Cancel on iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Briques, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancel on Android

  1. Open the Play Store and tap your profile icon.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Briques, then Cancel subscription.

What happens after you cancel

  • You keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for.
  • After that you move to Free. Trackers beyond the Free limit stay safe but become read-only drafts until you're back under the limit or resubscribe.
  • Any credit-pack credits you bought stay yours.
Deleting the app doesn't cancel billing. Removing Briques from your phone, or deleting your account, won't stop renewals — cancel through the store as above.
