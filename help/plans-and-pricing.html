Plans and pricing

← All help articles

Briques has two plans: Free and Pro. Both make single-user trackers — no seats, no per-user math.

Free — $0 forever

  • 1 active Brique (unlimited drafts)
  • 100 MB data, 5 MB file storage
  • Fast model
  • 80 AI credits per month
  • "Made with Briques" badge
  • Community support

Pro — $8.99 / month

  • 7 days free, then $8.99 / month
  • Unlimited Briques
  • 1 GB data, 500 MB file storage
  • Fast + Balanced models
  • 200 AI credits per month (roll over up to 2 months)
  • AI search inside your trackers, and AI workflows
  • Badge hidden, email support

Credit packs

Need more AI in a given month? One-time credit packs start at $2.99 and never expire. See how AI credits work.

Where to buy: Subscriptions and packs are purchased inside the app through Apple (iOS) or Google (Android). The pricing page has the full breakdown.
