Making trackers

Make your tracker live

← All help articles

A tracker has two states: draft and active. You set it up in draft, then make it live to use it for real.

Going live

  1. Open the tracker and tap Make live.
  2. Briques checks the tracker and switches it to active. Your pages now run as a working tracker, with real data.

Editing an active tracker

To change an active tracker, tap Back to draft to return it to draft. Make your edits in the builder, then make it live again. Your data stays put either way — only the tracker's structure is editable in draft.

Plan limits

How many trackers you can have active at once depends on your plan: one on Free, unlimited on Pro. Drafts don't count against the limit. See plans and pricing for the details.
