What is Briques?

← All help articles

Briques is a personal database tool for small, single-user trackers — the kind of thing you'd otherwise keep in a spreadsheet or a notes file. Describe what you want in plain language and Briques makes a working tracker, with screens, data, and logic. No code, ever, and no templates to wrestle with.

What you can make

Personal tools, mostly: a workout log, a client tracker, a reading list, an expense book, an inventory for a small shop. Anything that's a few screens over some structured data. We call each one a Brique.

What makes it different

  • Private by default. Your trackers and their data are yours alone. Nothing is shared, published, or distributed anywhere.
  • Real data, not mockups. Your trackers store actual records you add, edit, and search.
  • Yours to keep. Export your data any time, and cancel without losing what you made.

Where it runs

Briques is a mobile app for iOS and Android. You make and use your trackers right on your phone.
