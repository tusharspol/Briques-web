FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Briques an app builder? +

No. Briques is a personal database tool, like Notion or Airtable, just mobile-first and made for one person. Nothing you make is published or distributed anywhere; it runs only inside Briques, for you.

Do I need to code? +

Never. Describe what you want in plain English, or set up the tables yourself by hand. Either way, no code.

Who can see my Briques? +

Only you. Briques is single-user by design, with no sharing, feeds, or collaborators.

Can I change a Brique after I make it? +

Anytime. Add a field, rename a column, switch how you view it, or ask in plain English to adjust it. Your structure is never locked.
