Questions

Notion alternative FAQ.

Is Briques a good Notion alternative? +

If you want Notion-style databases but personal, mobile-first, and set up for you, yes. Briques focuses on single-user trackers you describe in plain English, rather than a full team workspace.

What's the difference between Briques and Notion? +

Notion is a collaborative workspace for docs, wikis, and databases you assemble yourself. Briques is a personal app builder: you describe what to track and it generates a focused mobile app in about a minute.

Is there a free Notion alternative? +

Briques has a free plan with one active Brique and 100 MB of data. Pro is $8.99 a month for unlimited Briques and more storage.

Is Briques a mobile-first alternative to Notion? +

Yes. Briques is designed phone-first. Your trackers live on iOS and Android and are made for a single person, with no desktop setup required.
